Phishing Detection Guide

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers create fake websites, emails, or messages to steal sensitive information.

  • Check website URL carefully before login.
  • Always verify HTTPS security.
  • Avoid clicking unknown email links.
  • Look for spelling mistakes in domains.
  • Never share passwords or OTP codes.
  • Be careful with urgent verification requests.
